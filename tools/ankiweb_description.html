AnkiHelper: fix the card you're studying in one key, and let an AI learn how you like your cards. This add-on is the Anki side of AnkiHelper, a free desktop app that turns notes you dictate or paste while studying into well-formed cards (following Andy Matuschak's "How to write good prompts", Michael Nielsen's "Augmenting long-term memory", SuperMemo's "Twenty rules of formulating knowledge" and Bloom's taxonomy), using an AI agent you already have: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or OpenCode. What the add-on does
  • F on the review screen: say what's wrong with the card ("too long", "ask why instead", "add an example") and it is rewritten in place. Edit → Undo brings the old card back.
  • Shift+F: flag the card red and keep studying; the next AnkiHelper batch rewrites it.
  • History of this card (right-click while reviewing): your original note and pictures, the other cards made from it, and the alternative versions you were offered, with one click to switch.
  • Learns from you. It notices the cards you edit and delete and suggests rules (for example "keep answers to one line") that every future card follows. Nothing becomes a rule without your OK.
  • An AnkiHelper button in the top bar opens the app: inbox, card preview and settings.
Requirements
  • The AnkiHelper app installed on the same computer: see the install guide. Run ankihelper install-addon once so the add-on can find it.
  • AnkiConnect for batches.
  • One AI agent CLI (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or OpenCode), logged in.
Fix and History work on any card with a question and an answer field; cloze cards are not supported yet. Linux is supported today; macOS and Windows support in the app is written but untested. Source, issues and feedback: github.com/da5ater/AnkiHelper
